About me

Security isn't a feature — it's a discipline.

I'm Albin Basil, a cybersecurity professional with hands-on experience across vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, security monitoring and incident response. My work sits at the intersection of offensive testing and defensive engineering — breaking things ethically to make them stronger.

I've assessed web applications, internal networks and cloud environments, and I'm equally comfortable in a SIEM triaging alerts at 2 a.m. or writing the report that helps a team fix root causes. I believe in clear communication, reproducible findings and remediation that actually ships.

Outside of engagements, I build labs, sharpen skills on TryHackMe, publish tooling on GitHub, and write about detection engineering and threat hunting.
